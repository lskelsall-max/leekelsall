Reading & Listening

The books, letters, podcasts, and essays that have most shaped how I think — as an operator, investor, and thinker.

I came to investing through operating, and to operating through law. That path — frontier market project finance, then building travel businesses from scratch in Africa — taught me that the most useful mental models sit at the intersection of disciplines most people keep separate. This page is an honest record of what I actually read and return to: not a curated shelf for appearances, but the sources that have changed how I see capital, organisations, markets, and the physical world. If you're trying to understand how I think, this is the most direct route.
